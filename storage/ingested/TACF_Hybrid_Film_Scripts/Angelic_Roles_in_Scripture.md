### **1. The Fallen Angel View (Traditional Hebrew & Early Christian Interpretation)**

- This interpretation suggests that fallen angels **left their proper domain** and had offspring (Nephilim) with human women.

- **Problem with this View:**  
  - Angels are spiritual beings, and **Jesus said angels do not marry** (Matthew 22:30).
  - However, some argue that **fallen angels could take physical form**, as seen when angels ate and interacted with humans in **Genesis 18-19**.
